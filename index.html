Staged filtering

Add filtering methods, arrange their order, and choose how many alternatives should survive. Each method filters the survivors from the previous step.

Available methods: Maximax, Maximin, Minimax Regret, and EMV/Bayes. Use the arrows to change the order and × to remove a method. Ties are always retained, so the final number may be higher than the target.
